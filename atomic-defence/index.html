Privacy Policy — Atomic Defence

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Atomic Defence does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data whatsoever.

What the app does not do

  • It has no internet access. The app does not declare the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not request or access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or device identifiers.
  • It contains no third-party SDKs.

What is stored on your device

The game saves two things locally, in the app's own private storage on your device, using standard browser local storage inside the app:

  • Your high score table — the three initials you type, the score, the wave reached and the difficulty played.
  • Your settings — sound on or off, haptics on or off, and difficulty.

This information never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by any other app. Uninstalling Atomic Defence deletes it permanently.

Permissions

The app requests a single permission, VIBRATE, which is used only to produce haptic feedback during play. It grants no access to any information. You can turn haptics off in the game's menu at any time.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
