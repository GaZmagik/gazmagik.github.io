Six cities. One battery. Wave after wave of warheads coming down.

Tap the sky to detonate flak, catch the incoming rounds before they land, and chain your blasts so one shot takes three. Flak is limited and the wave will not wait for you.

Nine things trying to get past you

Warheads that split at low altitude. Dodgers that swerve away from your blasts. Armoured heavies that shed their plating before they die. Hunters that ignore the cities entirely and eat your rounds in mid-air. Shielded warheads with a rotating plate you have to shoot around. Phantoms that fade out of sight between explosions. Burrowers that bury themselves instead of detonating, and give you five seconds to dig them out. Bomber patrols, and carrier airships that take three hits to bring down.
